## About me

I work at the intersection of customer behavior, go-to-market strategy, technology, and execution.

Over 15+ years, I have helped scale consumer acquisition, investigated how growth systems can be exploited, and built B2B demand generation engines across beauty subscriptions, technology platforms, cybersecurity, and insurance.

These days, I am especially interested in what happens when marketers stop treating AI as a writing tool and start using it as an operating layer: for research, workflow automation, rapid prototyping, and better decisions.

## Career highlights

- **Coalition:** Built B2B demand generation from SMB to enterprise and helped grow influenced pipeline from $10M to $100M+ across multiple international markets.
- **Meta:** Worked in Business Integrity Research Operations as a white-hat growth operator, finding product and advertising vulnerabilities before bad actors could exploit them.
- **ipsy:** Led paid acquisition and helped scale the beauty subscription business from roughly 700,000 to 2.5 million subscribers.
- **Startup ecosystem:** Mentored early-stage founders on demand generation and go-to-market through Race Capital and FoundersHK.

## How I work

- Start with the customer and the real operating constraint.
- Turn ambiguous problems into testable systems.
- Prefer evidence and working outcomes over hype.
- Build the smallest useful version, verify it, then improve it.
- Use automation to increase human judgment, not replace it.
